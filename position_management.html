What does position management do?

Turning the binary buy/sell action into a graded, risk-controlled position.

Start ¥100,000 · test window 2024-01-02 → 2026-01-09

Off vs On

Without position management
Buy / Sell

Binary action — you are either fully in (100%) or fully out (0%). No sizing, no automatic risk limits.

With position management
Hold 0–100% (or short)

The agent chooses how much to hold each day and adds stop-loss, drawdown lock and volatility scaling.

The four things it does

Graded sizing

Hold any size, not just all-in / all-out.

e.g. Moderate signal → hold 60%, not 100%.

Volatility scaling

Bigger size when calm, smaller when choppy.

e.g. Low volatility → scale the target position up; high → shrink it.

Stop-loss

Cut a losing trade automatically.

e.g. Trade down 5% → sell to cash immediately.

Drawdown lock

Protect the account after a slump.

e.g. Account down 15% from peak → stop adding, only reduce.
