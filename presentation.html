Environment Design

Two generations of market simulation

04 / 12
V1 / ShanghaiIndexEnvT1

Discrete T+1 trading

  • Action space: Buy or Sell, based on gym-anytrading primitives.
  • T+1 compliance: long positions must be held at least one day before selling.
  • Observation: 30-day window over 10 technical signal features.
Buy/SellT+1Fees
V2 / PositionMgmtEnv

Continuous position management

  • Action space: target net position ratio in [-1, 1].
  • Observation: 13 technical features plus 6 portfolio state features.
  • Built-in risk controls for stop-loss, drawdown lock, fees, and volatility scaling.

Reward and Risk Control

Reward the tradeoff, not only profit

06 / 12
R = sharpe_component x 1.0
  + drawdown_penalty x 0.5
  + turnover_penalty x 0.1

The V2 reward uses Differential Sharpe Ratio as a per-step signal, then penalizes current drawdown and unnecessary position churn.

5%

Stop-loss flattening

Forced exposure reduction when unrealized loss breaches the configured threshold.

15%

Max drawdown lock

Restricts new exposure after portfolio drawdown crosses the hard limit.

ATR

Volatility scaling

Targets smaller positions in high-volatility regimes and larger ones in calm regimes.

Fee

Transaction cost drag

Each position change pays explicit trading cost before reward is computed.

Evaluation Metrics

Performance judged by return quality and risk

07 / 12

Sharpe Ratio

Annualized excess return per unit of volatility.

Sortino Ratio

Downside-risk version of Sharpe for asymmetric losses.

Max Drawdown

Worst peak-to-trough equity decline.

Calmar Ratio

Annualized return divided by maximum drawdown.

Win Rate

Share of completed trades with positive return.

Turnover

Average absolute position change per step.

Baseline

Random Strategy

Every trained agent is compared against a random policy over repeated evaluation episodes.
