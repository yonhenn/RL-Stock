Why SAC — the requirements

Action space

Continuous sizing

Target position a∈[-1,1] rules out discrete methods; needs a continuous-control algorithm.

Data + reward

Noisy & scarce

Few years of daily bars and a noisy Sharpe-style reward favour off-policy replay (sample reuse) over on-policy PPO.

Exploration

Entropy helps

SAC's max-entropy objective keeps exploring position sizes and is robust to hyper-parameters — the natural fit.
